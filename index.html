SOUP Tools

A free Windows desktop app for Valorant teams: it saves your scrims and custom games, keeps a library of replays, shows stats and heatmaps, and plays replays back in 3D. It runs on your own PC and talks to Riot's client on that PC.

Download for Windows

Drive Sync

Drive Sync is SOUP Tools' official extension for Google Drive. Sign in with Google to back up your replay library and your SOUP settings to your own Google Drive, restore them on another PC, and share a team folder so everyone's replays stay in sync.

It only sees files it creates in your Drive or that you choose to open with it, nothing else. See the privacy policy.
